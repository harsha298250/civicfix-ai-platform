Reporting Guidelines

Field crews prioritize reports with actionable geographic clarity and photo evidence.

  • photo_camera

    High-Contrast Framing

    Include an object (like a water bottle) or clear road line to convey scale and depth.

  • share_location

    Exact Intersections

    Specify lane direction (e.g., "eastbound curb lane") to keep utility crews safe during dispatch.

  • privacy_tip

    Protect Private Privacy

    Do not capture vehicle license plates, faces of passersby, or private residential interiors.
